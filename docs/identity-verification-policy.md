# Affiliation verification

Student and educator claims are separate roles. An administrator's manual review records method, reviewer, timestamp, reason and expiry. Student affiliation lasts 180 days; educator affiliation lasts 365 days. Renewal requires another administrator review. Revocation removes the active badge immediately.

Public profiles expose only currently verified, unexpired roles with recorded review provenance. Old verified records without provenance or expiry require review before they qualify for a public badge. Own claim/history reads retain the original review status; clients must also check expiry before displaying active verification.

Store no identity document or sensitive uploaded evidence in this claim model. Review reasons should describe the method and decision, not retain document numbers, addresses or scans. Manual reviewers must inspect evidence through an approved minimized-evidence process. This backend does not claim automated institutional verification or certify the operational review procedure.

Identity review does not review educational accuracy. Content quality review belongs to a specific lesson version; ranking and moderation must never substitute a student or educator badge for content review.
