# Code of Conduct

Chaos follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept feedback about the work rather than the person, and don't harass, insult or exclude anyone. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, email the maintainers at the support address on [chaos.fail](https://chaos.fail) (the Contact link in the footer). Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or ban people who break these rules.
